Why LumiFair exists

I've spent more than ten years building software in the corporate and public sector: frontline IT support, security, large modernization projects, and later as a managing partner at a software consultancy, hiring and training developers and leading teams.

That work taught me how good software gets built. Start small. Involve the people who will use it. Leave the team able to run it themselves. It also taught me that why you do the work matters as much as how.

LumiFair is how I put that experience to work for organizations doing work that truly matters.
